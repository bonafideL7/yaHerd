# Milestone 12 — Direct Animal Core Data read-graph integrity

## Design and scope

The asynchronous M9 Animal read model validates Herd-owned relationships before hydrating summary and Dashboard projections. The direct Core Data Animal repository resolves the selected Animal by Herd but maps its parent, pasture, tag, status-reference, history and maternal-offspring relationships without the equivalent guard. A malformed persisted relationship can therefore disclose another Herd's names/IDs or produce wrong detail/timeline data. The direct list, detail, timeline, aggregate-editor, parent options and offspring draft-seed paths share the defect category.

**Design:** Extract the existing M9 Animal relationship check into one Data-layer, persistence-only graph validator, extend it to all directly projected parent/child edges (including reverse owner checks), and invoke it before direct read mapping and M9's hydrated reads and asynchronous parent-option lookup. Keep lightweight type classification's existing narrow early check. Do not change Core Data schema, writes, SwiftData, navigation, synchronization, recovery, UI, sorting, or existing Domain snapshots. Read-only corruption must fail without repair.

**Ownership:** This PR owns cross-Herd and reverse-owner integrity of direct Animal read projections and the shared M9 validator. M5 remains the contract owner for Animal mutations, identities and legal history; M9 owns pagination/filters/Dashboard behavior; prior M12 animal timeline and pregnancy matrices own ordering/derived identity; M7/M8 own Field Check/Working writes. Legacy SwiftData release-upgrade preservation remains unresolved and `Info/CORE_DATA_CUTOVER.md` must remain.
