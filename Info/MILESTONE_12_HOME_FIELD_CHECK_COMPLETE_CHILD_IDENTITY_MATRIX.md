# Milestone 12 — Home Field Check complete-Herd child identity

## Production trace and design (before editing production code)

`LoadHomeUseCase.execute` independently invokes `HomeFieldCheckQueryReading.fetchHomeFieldCheckRecords()`; `HomeViewModel` displays warning sessions, unresolved count/preview and `hasHistory`. `CoreDataReadModelActor` already preflights **all** selected-Herd session UUIDs (#176) but scans finding IDs only for unresolved records and checks finding/check identities of **warning sessions** only. Resolved findings and animal checks attached to completed, non-warning sessions can reuse a visible warning child's UUID. Home then renders that warning card, while direct `CoreDataFieldCheckRepository.fetchSessions()` and `fetchSessionDetail(id:)` reject that same ambiguous physical identity across the entire Herd. The independently scoped Home operation must agree on entity identity.

Within the existing read-only private context and pinned `.current` generation, expand the existing finding dictionary-ID scan to **all selected-Herd findings regardless of status or session completion** and add an ID-only selected-Herd scan for `CDFieldCheckAnimalCheck`. Retain the existing corrupt finding status/relationship probe. The existing warning graph validators remain the owner of warning child ownership and session attachment. Reuse `CoreDataPersistenceError.duplicateApplicationID(entity:id:herdID:)`; no mutations, rekey, new abstraction, expanded child hydration, sync, SwiftData, schema or selection changes. The extra scan is O(selected-Herd check count); the finding scan broadens from unresolved to all findings and runtime cost is not benchmarked.

**Contract ownership:** This PR owns the **independent Home reader's** complete-Herd child-UUID preflight (findings and animal checks) and its durable SQLite cross-boundary regression. Direct Field Check M7/M12 (#159) owns the canonical full-Herd child identity and relationship graph; M9 owns Home warning predicate/count/preview/history projections; M12 #172 owns duplicate findings inside warning sessions, and #176 owns full-Herd session UUID preflight. Historical non-warning child relationship validation beyond identity is owned by direct Field Check and not added to Home.
