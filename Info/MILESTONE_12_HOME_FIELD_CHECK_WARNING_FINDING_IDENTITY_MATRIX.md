# Milestone 12 — Home Field Check Warning-Session Finding UUID Identity

## Design and contract ownership

The direct `CoreDataFieldCheckRepository.validateFieldCheckIdentity` checks every selected-Herd `CDFieldCheckFinding` UUID across sessions before direct session/read projections. The independent Home `CoreDataReadModelActor.fetchHomeFieldCheckRecords` already rejects duplicate **unresolved** finding IDs through its complete selected-Herd aggregation predicate and rejects duplicates **within** each warning session. It does not reject duplicate **resolved** finding UUIDs across two different fetched warning sessions. Both warning cards can therefore display while direct Field Check refuses to read the same source records.

Within the existing selected-Herd warning-session cohort (unfinished sessions and sessions with pending checks/findings), validate all pre-fetched `CDFieldCheckFinding` application UUIDs together **before** mapping Home summaries. Reuse `CoreDataAnimalMutation.validateUniqueApplicationIDs` and the existing `CoreDataPersistenceError.duplicateApplicationID`. Do not fetch all historical findings a second time, expand the Home session query, alter unresolved counting semantics, mutate physical records, or introduce a new validator. Home remains a bounded-by-predicate summary, not a replacement for direct Field Check's complete selected-Herd integrity gate. Distinct Herds may reuse a UUID.

- **This PR:** cross-session UUID identity of all pre-fetched findings from Home's selected warning sessions, including resolved historical findings.
- **Existing M9/Home contract:** unresolved finding count/one-finding preview, historical `hasHistory`, warning-session selection and sort, query-generation consistency.
- **Direct Field Check / M12 #159:** complete selected-Herd child identity and foreign-Herd relationship integrity; direct session detail/list and mutation semantics.
- **Other M12 Home contracts:** animal-check cross-warning-session identity and unresolved-finding global identity; no duplicate contract fixtures.
- **Excluded:** inactive/non-warning historical sessions, data repair, writes, status transitions, CloudKit, SwiftData migration, schema, navigation, new concurrency or verification entry points.
